Attaché – Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

In short: Attaché works entirely inside Outlook on your device. It does not send your emails, attachments, or any other data to us or to anyone else, and it has no server, account, analytics, or advertising.

What the add-in accesses

When you open the Attaché pane on an email, the add-in reads, through Outlook's add-in interface, information about the currently open message only: the names, sizes, and types of its attachments and, if you use the search box, the contents of those attachments so that it can find your search words inside them. It requests the minimum permission Outlook offers for this (ReadItem). It cannot send, move, edit, or delete email, and it cannot read messages other than the one you have open.

How that information is used

Everything is processed locally in the add-in pane, in your device's memory. Text is extracted from attachments on your device (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, and plain-text files) purely to power the search you type. That text is not saved to disk, is not transmitted anywhere, and is discarded when you switch to another email or close the pane. If you choose to download an attachment, it is saved to your device by your browser or Outlook, exactly as if you had downloaded it from Outlook directly.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, sell, share, or have access to your emails, attachments, search terms, or usage. The add-in does not use cookies, tracking, analytics, or advertising, and it does not require you to create an account.

Third parties and hosting

  • GitHub Pages hosts the add-in's files (its web page, scripts, and icons). Like any website host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your Outlook client loads those files. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Microsoft provides Outlook and the office.js library the add-in loads from appsforoffice.microsoft.com. Microsoft may collect diagnostic data under its own terms; see Microsoft's privacy statement. We do not control this.
  • The add-in's other code, including the libraries used to read Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF files, is bundled with the add-in and served from the same host. No third-party content-delivery network receives your data.

Security

All files are served over HTTPS, and the add-in bundles its own libraries rather than loading them from third-party servers. The add-in contains no code that sends your emails or attachments anywhere.

Retention

We keep no data, so there is nothing to retain or delete. Removing the add-in from Outlook removes it completely.

Children

The add-in is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the add-in's behavior changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
